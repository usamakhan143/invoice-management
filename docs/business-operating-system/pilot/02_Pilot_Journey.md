# 02 — Pilot Journey

**Business:** Mobile App Development Agency  
**Initiative:** Digikinz Client Acquisition System  
**Perspective:** Founder journey from idea to scale  
**Purpose:** Map the complete real-world journey—not software features

---

## 1. Journey Overview

The Digikinz initiative is not “run Meta Ads.” It is a **complete acquisition operating system**: brand, offer, funnel, CRM, automation, paid media, human sales, delivery handoff, revenue, learning, and scale.

```
Idea → Brand → Offer → Landing Page → Automation → Meta Campaign
  → Lead → Meeting → Proposal → Client → Project Delivery → Payment → Referral → Scale
```

Each stage below is a **business stage**, not a BOS module.

---

## 2. Stage-by-Stage Journey

### Stage 1 — Idea

**What happens:** Founder decides to build a repeatable client acquisition system for the mobile app agency, anchored on the Digikinz positioning.

**Outputs:** Strategic intent, rough thesis, venture context (“this is how we grow the agency”).

**Key actors:** Founder.

**Success signal:** Initiative chartered with hypothesis and budget envelope.

---

### Stage 2 — Brand

**What happens:** Name, visual identity, messaging, proof assets, tone of voice for Digikinz (or agency sub-brand).

**Outputs:** Brand kit, logo, colors, tagline, case study templates.

**Key actors:** Founder, designer, copywriter.

**Success signal:** Brand usable across landing page, ads, and sales conversations.

---

### Stage 3 — Offer

**What happens:** Define what is sold, to whom, at what price point, with what guarantee/deliverables.

**Outputs:** Offer document, pricing tiers, scope boundaries, ICP definition.

**Key actors:** Founder, sales lead.

**Success signal:** Offer is clear enough to put on a landing page and use in proposals.

---

### Stage 4 — Landing Page

**What happens:** Build conversion surface (page, form, calendar embed, thank-you flow).

**Outputs:** Live URL, conversion events, tracking pixels.

**Key actors:** Founder, developer/marketer.

**Success signal:** Page live; form submissions flow into CRM/automation.

---

### Stage 5 — Automation

**What happens:** CRM + workflow automation (e.g., GoHighLevel): lead capture, nurture sequences, SMS/email, booking links, pipeline stages.

**Outputs:** Pipelines, automations, notifications, setter/closer handoff rules.

**Key actors:** Founder, ops/automation specialist.

**Success signal:** Lead → nurture → meeting booking works without manual chaos.

---

### Stage 6 — Meta Campaign

**What happens:** Ad account setup, creative production, audience targeting, campaign launch, daily optimization.

**Outputs:** Active campaigns, ad spend, creative variants, UTM-tagged traffic.

**Key actors:** Founder, media buyer.

**Success signal:** Stable CPL; leads entering automation/CRM.

---

### Stage 7 — Lead

**What happens:** Prospect enters system via form, ad lead form, or manual entry.

**Outputs:** CRM lead record, source tags, campaign attribution.

**Key actors:** System, setter (optional).

**Success signal:** Lead counted, attributed to Meta (or channel), enters pipeline.

---

### Stage 8 — Meeting

**What happens:** Discovery call / strategy session booked and held.

**Outputs:** Meeting held (yes/no), notes, qualification outcome.

**Key actors:** Setter, closer, founder.

**Success signal:** Qualified meetings on calendar; unqualified leads filtered.

---

### Stage 9 — Proposal

**What happens:** Scope, timeline, price packaged and sent.

**Outputs:** Proposal sent, value quoted, follow-up sequence.

**Key actors:** Closer, founder.

**Success signal:** Proposal in client’s hands; pipeline stage advanced.

---

### Stage 10 — Client

**What happens:** Deal closed; prospect becomes paying client.

**Outputs:** Signed agreement, CRM customer, project kickoff trigger.

**Key actors:** Closer, founder, delivery lead.

**Success signal:** Client count increases; acquisition initiative produces revenue opportunity.

---

### Stage 11 — Project Delivery

**What happens:** Mobile app scoped, designed, built, launched for the client.

**Outputs:** Deliverables, milestones, delivery expenses, time (internal).

**Key actors:** PM, developers, designers, client.

**Success signal:** Client product shipped; satisfaction sufficient for testimonial/referral.

**Note:** This is **post-acquisition** work. It affects **profitability** and **referrals**, but is not the same as the acquisition initiative itself.

---

### Stage 12 — Payment

**What happens:** Invoices issued and collected (deposit + milestones or full project fee).

**Outputs:** Invoice records, cash in bank, revenue recognized operationally.

**Key actors:** Finance, founder.

**Success signal:** Revenue attributed back to acquisition effort for ROI.

---

### Stage 13 — Referral

**What happens:** Satisfied client or network introduces new lead.

**Outputs:** Referral lead in CRM, often lower CAC.

**Key actors:** Client, founder, sales.

**Success signal:** New lead with source = Referral; optional loop back to Meeting stage.

---

### Stage 14 — Scale

**What happens:** Founder doubles down (more ad spend, hire closers, clone funnel for new ICP) or pivots (new offer, new channel, kill initiative).

**Outputs:** Budget decisions, new experiments, initiative closure or successor initiative.

**Key actors:** Founder.

**Success signal:** ROI positive → scale; ROI negative → pivot or kill with lesson captured.

---

## 3. Parallel Workstreams (Cross-Cutting)

These run across multiple stages—not sequential steps:

| Workstream | Touches stages | Nature |
|------------|----------------|--------|
| **Spend / investment** | Brand, Landing, Automation, Meta, Sales tools | Continuous outflows |
| **Decision log** | Offer, Meta, Pricing, Hire closer, Scale/Kill | Strategic choices |
| **KPI monitoring** | All stages after Meta launch | Feedback loops |
| **Attribution hygiene** | Lead, Payment, Spend | Links ERP facts to initiative |
| **Risk & assumptions** | Offer, Meta, Close rate expectations | Explicit bets |

---

## 4. Initiative Timeline (Illustrative)

| Phase | Stages | Typical duration |
|-------|--------|------------------|
| **Build** | Idea → Automation | Weeks 1–4 |
| **Launch** | Meta Campaign → Lead | Week 5+ |
| **Convert** | Meeting → Client | Weeks 5–12 |
| **Monetize** | Payment | Overlaps convert |
| **Deliver** | Project Delivery | Post-close |
| **Learn** | Referral → Scale | Ongoing |

---

## 5. Journey Exit Conditions

The **Digikinz Client Acquisition System** initiative closes when the founder:

- **Succeeds:** System hits success criteria (e.g., N clients at target CAC/ROI) → scale decision.
- **Pivots:** Offer or channel fails → successor initiative (linked via `predecessorInitiativeId`).
- **Kills:** Bet invalid → closed with lesson learned (required if investment > 0 per Doc 11).
