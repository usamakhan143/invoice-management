================================================================================
BUSINESS OPERATING SYSTEM (BOS)
Document 13 — Decision Engine
================================================================================

Authoring perspective: Principal Enterprise Software Architect
Status: Architecture phase — core institutional memory design
Version: 1.0

The Decision Engine is not a feature. It is a core asset of the BOS —
the mechanism that preserves WHY the business chose a path, not just WHAT happened.

Without it, the BOS is a reporting layer.
With it, the BOS becomes institutional memory that compounds across decades.

================================================================================
1. PURPOSE
================================================================================

Problems the Decision Engine solves:

    1. "Why did we kill that initiative?" — answered 2 years later
    2. "We tried this before and it failed" — but nobody remembers the details
    3. "I would have decided differently" — but alternatives were never recorded
    4. "The ROI looked good on paper" — but expected vs actual was never compared
    5. "New hire repeats old mistakes" — no searchable decision history

The Decision Engine ensures every significant strategic choice is:
    • Recorded with context
    • Linked to initiatives, ventures, and KPIs
    • Evaluated against actual outcomes
    • Searchable for future ventures


================================================================================
2. WHAT QUALIFIES AS A DECISION
================================================================================

A BosDecision should be recorded when:

    MUST record:
        • Initiative activated, paused, closed, killed, or pivoted
        • Budget increase/decrease > 20% on active initiative
        • Channel allocation change (major reallocation)
        • New venture launched or venture archived
        • Attribution void or major correction
        • Experiment adoption (roll out winner) or abandonment
        • Playbook published

    SHOULD record:
        • Pricing change for an offer
        • ICP/market segment change
        • Hire vs outsource choice
        • Tool/platform selection (GHL vs alternative)
        • Continue vs kill when ROI negative but leads positive

    DO NOT record (noise):
        • Individual expense entry
        • Individual lead assignment
        • Routine weekly tasks
        • UI preference changes

Rule: If the choice affects initiative ROI, venture direction, or budget >
      $500 equivalent — record a BosDecision.

3. DECISION ENTITY MODEL (CONCEPTUAL)
================================================================================

BosDecision
    ├── id
    ├── companyId
    ├── ventureId(s)              — Which venture(s) affected
    ├── initiativeId(s)           — Which initiative(s) affected
    ├── experimentId (optional)
    ├── decisionType              — Enum (see section 4)
    ├── title                     — Short summary
    ├── context                   — Situation at time of decision
    ├── decision                  — What was decided (the choice made)
    ├── rationale                 — Why this choice (the reasoning)
    ├── alternatives[]            — BosDecisionAlternative records
    ├── expectedOutcome           — What we expect to happen
    ├── expectedKpiImpact[]       — Which KPIs should move, how
    ├── actualOutcome (later)     — What actually happened
    ├── actualKpiSnapshot[] (later) — KPI values at evaluation time
    ├── confidenceLevel           — LOW | MEDIUM | HIGH
    ├── reversibility             — EASY | MODERATE | IRREVERSIBLE
    ├── ownerId                   — Who made/owns the decision
    ├── approverId (optional)     — Who approved if different
    ├── relatedDecisionIds[]      — Prior decisions this builds on or supersedes
    ├── supersededById (optional) — If this decision was replaced
    ├── status                    — Lifecycle state (see Document 11)
    ├── decidedAt                 — Timestamp
    ├── evaluatedAt (optional)    — When outcome was reviewed
    ├── lessonLearnedId (optional)— Link to BosLessonLearned on evaluation
    ├── tags[]                    — Searchable: channel, model, outcome
    └── attachments (optional)    — Links to notes, screenshots, external docs


BosDecisionAlternative
    ├── id
    ├── decisionId
    ├── title                     — Alternative option name
    ├── description               — What this alternative entailed
    ├── pros[]                    — Advantages
    ├── cons[]                    — Disadvantages
    ├── estimatedCost (optional)
    ├── estimatedOutcome (optional)
    ├── rejectedReason            — Why not chosen
    └── wasSelected               — boolean (exactly one true per decision)

5. DECISION LIFECYCLE (SUMMARY — FULL DETAIL IN DOC 11)
================================================================================

    PROPOSED ──► APPROVED ──► ACTIVE ──► EVALUATED
                    │           │
                    │           ├──► SUPERSEDED (by newer decision)
                    │           └──► REVOKED (rare)

Entry (ACTIVE):
    Decision is in effect. May trigger initiative state transitions.

Evaluation (EVALUATED):
    Required for decisions with expectedOutcome when:
        • Initiative closes
        • 90 days elapsed (configurable)
        • Founder manually triggers review

    Evaluation captures:
        • actualOutcome
        • actualKpiSnapshot
        • outcomeRating: BETTER_THAN_EXPECTED | AS_EXPECTED | WORSE_THAN_EXPECTED
        • optional BosLessonLearned auto-draft

6. DECISION RECOMMENDATION ENGINE (ADVISORY — NOT AUTONOMOUS)
================================================================================

The BOS may PROPOSE decisions based on KPI thresholds. It never executes
them without human approval (founder or authorized role).

Recommendation record:
    BosDecisionRecommendation
        ├── triggerType         — KPI_THRESHOLD | BUDGET_BURN | STALE_INITIATIVE
        ├── triggerEvidence     — KPI values that fired the trigger
        ├── suggestedDecisionType
        ├── suggestedTitle
        ├── suggestedRationale  — Auto-generated prose from KPI data
        ├── status              — PENDING | ACCEPTED | DISMISSED | EXPIRED
        └── linkedDecisionId    — If founder accepts and creates BosDecision

Example triggers:

    ROI < 0% for 60 days + investment > $1,000
        → Recommend: CLOSE_INITIATIVE_KILL or PIVOT_INITIATIVE

    Channel A ROI > 3× Channel B ROI
        → Recommend: REALLOCATE_CHANNEL_BUDGET

    Budget utilization > 80% with no revenue
        → Recommend: PAUSE_INITIATIVE or DECREASE_BUDGET

    Initiative active > 120 days with no leads
        → Recommend: Review ICP and channel (CHANGE_ICP or ADD_CHANNEL)

Critical rule:
    Recommendations create BosDecisionRecommendation records.
    They do NOT auto-close initiatives or void attributions.
    Human approval always required.

7. RELATIONSHIPS TO OTHER BOS ENTITIES
================================================================================

    BosDecision ──► BosInitiative     (many-to-many)
    BosDecision ──► BosVenture        (many-to-many)
    BosDecision ──► BosExperiment     (optional)
    BosDecision ──► BosMetricSnapshot (expected and actual KPIs)
    BosDecision ──► BosLessonLearned  (on evaluation)
    BosDecision ──► BosDecision       (supersedes / related chain)
    BosDecision ──► BosAssumption     (invalidate assumption on evaluation)
    BosDecision ──► BosRisk           (realize risk on evaluation)

Decision chain example:

    Decision D1: "Launch Mobile App Agency Q1 Acquisition" (ACTIVATE_INITIATIVE)
        └── Decision D2: "Increase LinkedIn budget to $1,500" (INCREASE_BUDGET)
            └── Decision D3: "Kill Google Ads experiment" (ABANDON_EXPERIMENT)
                └── Decision D4: "Close initiative as SUCCESS" (CLOSE_INITIATIVE)
                    └── Lesson L1: "LinkedIn outreach wins for agencies"

Future founder launching AI Agency sees D1-L1 chain when creating similar initiative.

8. INSTITUTIONAL MEMORY — SEARCH & DISCOVERY
================================================================================

Search dimensions:
    • By venture, initiative, channel, decision type
    • By tag (agency, saas, linkedin, failed, success)
    • By outcome rating (find past WORSE_THAN_EXPECTED decisions)
    • By KPI affected (all decisions that targeted CPL)
    • Full-text on rationale and context

Discovery features (phased):

    Phase 1: Manual search and filters
    Phase 2: "Similar decisions" when creating new initiative
    Phase 3: Auto-surface relevant lessons + decisions on initiative create
    Phase 4: Pattern analysis ("Decisions to increase budget without experiment
             evidence have 70% WORSE_THAN_EXPECTED rate")


================================================================================
9. DECISION vs LESSON LEARNED — BOUNDARY
================================================================================

    BosDecision               | BosLessonLearned
    --------------------------|------------------------------------------
    A choice made             | An insight derived
    Has alternatives          | Has recommendations
    Has expected outcome      | Has what worked / didn't work
    Evaluated for accuracy    | Published for reuse
    Point-in-time             | Timeless (until deprecated)

Flow:
    Decision (ACTIVE) → time passes → Evaluation (EVALUATED) → Lesson (PUBLISHED)

One decision may produce zero or one primary lesson.
One lesson may reference multiple decisions.


================================================================================
10. REPORTING & AUDIT REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

Reports the Decision Engine must support (future):

    • Decision log export (CSV) — full audit trail
    • Decision outcome accuracy — % BETTER / AS / WORSE than expected
    • Decision latency — time from PROPOSED to ACTIVE
    • Un evaluated decisions — decisions past due for review
    • Initiative decision history — all decisions for one initiative
    • Portfolio decision density — decisions per venture per quarter

Audit requirements:
    • No hard delete of BosDecision ever
    • All status changes append to decision audit trail
    • Supersede must link forward and backward IDs
    • Void attribution must reference a BosDecision of type VOID_ATTRIBUTION

================================================================================
12. WHY THIS IS CORE — NOT OPTIONAL
================================================================================

Documents 01–09 treated Decision Log as one module among many.
This document elevates it to a first-class pillar:

    BOS Pillars:
        1. Attribution (what happened, strategically contextualized)
        2. Measurement (KPIs, ROI, funnel)
        3. Decision Engine (why it happened, was it right)
        4. Institutional Memory (lessons, playbooks, assumptions)

Remove Decision Engine → BOS degrades to a fancy reporting dashboard.
Remove Attribution → BOS has no data.
Remove Measurement → BOS has no feedback loop.
Remove Institutional Memory → BOS does not compound across ventures.

All four pillars required.

13. ARCHITECTURAL CONSTRAINTS (FOR FUTURE IMPLEMENTATION)
================================================================================

    • BosDecision stored in BOS-owned collection — never in ERP documents
    • Decision creation does NOT trigger ERP side effects directly
    • Initiative state transitions may REQUIRE a BosDecision reference ID
    • KPI recommendation triggers read from BosMetricSnapshot — not live compute
    • Decision search indexed by ventureId, initiativeId, tags, decidedAt
