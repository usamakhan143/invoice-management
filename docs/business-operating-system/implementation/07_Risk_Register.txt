================================================================================
BOS IMPLEMENTATION VALIDATION
Document 07 — Risk Register
================================================================================

Purpose: Identified risks for BOS Phase 1 integration
Format: ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner

================================================================================
CRITICAL RISKS (P0)
================================================================================

R-001 | Expense schema pollution
      | Developer adds initiativeId to expenses document
      | Likelihood: Medium | Impact: Critical
      | Mitigation: Code review gate; bosAttributionService only write path;
        PR template checkbox; Firestore rules cannot block unknown fields on
        expenses but console audit after deploy
      | Owner: Reviewer

R-002 | CRM Business vs BosVenture naming collision
      | Team uses businessService or businesses collection for BOS ventures
      | Likelihood: Medium | Impact: Critical
      | Mitigation: bosVentureService mandatory; lint rule or grep in CI for
        wrong imports; Doc 10 naming in PR description
      | Owner: Implementer

R-003 | ExpensesPage god component — attribution breaks finance flows
      | Attribution UI bundled into 3,190-line file causes regressions
      | Likelihood: High | Impact: High
      | Mitigation: BLOCKER — split ExpenseDetailModal before Stream D (Doc 05)
      | Owner: Implementer

R-004 | Staff expense form changed unintentionally
      | Violates Option C UX; training disruption
      | Likelihood: Medium | Impact: High
      | Mitigation: Attribution only in detail modal; separate permission;
        BC checklist item E-01 through E-05 (Doc 08)
      | Owner: Product owner


================================================================================
HIGH RISKS (P1)
================================================================================

R-005 | firestore.rules deploy lag
      | Client writes to bos* before rules deployed → permission denied errors
      | Likelihood: Medium | Impact: High
      | Mitigation: Deploy rules + indexes before app; feature flag by permission
      | Owner: Firebase admin

R-006 | Permission default allow
      | Missing bos_* check defaults to visible actions
      | Likelihood: Medium | Impact: High
      | Mitigation: Explicit false in canBos*(); ProtectedComponent on all routes
      | Owner: Implementer

R-007 | Reports performance degradation
      | Extra queries + in-memory join on large expense sets
      | Likelihood: Low-Medium | Impact: Medium
      | Mitigation: Load attributions only when bos_reports_view; useMemo;
        monitor companies with >5k expenses
      | Owner: Implementer

R-008 | Backup v5 restore loses BOS data
      | User restores old backup after attributions created
      | Likelihood: Low | Impact: High
      | Mitigation: Bump backup version; UI warning; document in Data Management
      | Owner: Implementer

R-009 | Duplicate attribution per expense
      | Two initiatives linked to same expense
      | Likelihood: Medium | Impact: Medium
      | Mitigation: Application check in bosAttributionService.create;
        query existing before insert
      | Owner: Implementer

R-010 | Initiative vs Campaign confusion
      | Users create BosInitiative thinking it replaces Campaign
      | Likelihood: Medium | Impact: Medium
      | Mitigation: UI labels "Strategic initiative" not "Campaign"; help text
      | Owner: Product owner


================================================================================
MEDIUM RISKS (P2)
================================================================================

R-011 | usePermissions.tsx growth
      | 700+ lines becomes unmaintainable
      | Likelihood: High | Impact: Low-Medium
      | Mitigation: Extract useBosPermissions in Stream B
      | Owner: Implementer

R-012 | Firestore index missing in production
      | Composite query fails at runtime
      | Likelihood: Medium | Impact: Medium
      | Mitigation: firestore.indexes.json in repo; deploy checklist
      | Owner: Firebase admin

R-013 | ActivityType enum incomplete
      | BOS events show raw type strings in ActivityPage
      | Likelihood: Medium | Impact: Low
      | Mitigation: Add all bos_* types with labels in ActivityPage map
      | Owner: Implementer

R-014 | Cross-company data leak via wrong companyId
      | Bug in service passes wrong companyId
      | Likelihood: Low | Impact: Critical
      | Mitigation: Always resolve via resolveCompanyIdForUser; never trust client
        companyId from form without profile check
      | Owner: Implementer

R-015 | Net investment calculation inconsistency
      | Initiative spend report uses gross expense vs net after returns
      | Likelihood: Medium | Impact: Medium
      | Mitigation: Document P1 as gross; Phase 2 align with Reports net column
      | Owner: Product owner

R-016 | HashRouter deep links to BOS pages
      | Bookmark /bos/initiatives/:id fails if permission revoked
      | Likelihood: Low | Impact: Low
      | Mitigation: ProtectedComponent redirect to dashboard
      | Owner: Implementer
