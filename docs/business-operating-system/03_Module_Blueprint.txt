================================================================================
BUSINESS OPERATING SYSTEM (BOS)
Document 03 — Module Blueprint
================================================================================

Authoring perspective: Principal ERP Architect · Business Systems Consultant
Status: Business blueprint — pre-implementation
Version: 1.0

This document defines every module the BOS should eventually contain.
Nothing listed here is implemented. Each entry describes PURPOSE only.

Modules are grouped by strategic function, not by technical layer.

================================================================================
GROUP A — FOUNDATION (Who and What)
================================================================================

MODULE A1: Business Portfolio
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
    The top-level container for every venture the founder operates. Each business
    (Mobile App Agency, AI Automation Agency, SaaS Product X) is a distinct entity
    within the portfolio, with its own initiatives, channels, and P&L context.

Answers:
    "What businesses am I running?"
    "Which business does this initiative belong to?"

Examples:
    • Mobile App Development Agency
    • AI Automation Agency
    • Cold Outreach SaaS Tool
    • Marketing Agency

Future expansion:
    Business units within a business, franchise models, joint ventures.


MODULE A2: Business Model Registry
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
    Defines the type and economics of each business — not the business itself,
    but its structural pattern. Enables cross-business comparison and lesson transfer.

Answers:
    "What kind of business is this?"
    "What economics should I expect?"

Examples:
    • Service Agency (project-based revenue)
    • SaaS (recurring subscription revenue)
    • Product Business (one-time sales)
    • Marketplace (commission-based)
    • Info Product (course/digital product)

Future expansion:
    Benchmark data per model type, industry templates.


================================================================================
GROUP B — STRATEGY (Why and Where)
================================================================================

MODULE B1: Growth Initiatives
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
    The central strategic unit of the BOS. A Growth Initiative represents a
    deliberate, time-bound effort to achieve a specific business outcome —
    launch a new service, enter a new market, test a new channel, build an MVP.

    This is the HEART of the BOS. Most other modules exist to support initiatives.

Answers:
    "What am I trying to achieve?"
    "Why am I investing in this?"
    "Is it working?"

Examples:
    • "Launch Mobile App Agency — Q1 2026 Client Acquisition"
    • "Validate AI Automation SaaS MVP"
    • "Test LinkedIn Cold Outreach for Agency Leads"
    • "Build Personal Brand via Content Marketing"

See Document 05 for full Growth Initiative Blueprint.

Future expansion:
    Initiative templates, playbook library, cross-initiative dependencies.


MODULE B2: Acquisition Channels
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
    Defines HOW customers/leads are acquired for each initiative. Channels are
    reusable across initiatives and businesses.

Answers:
    "How am I getting customers?"
    "Which channel is performing best?"

Examples:
    • Cold Email Outreach
    • LinkedIn Direct Messages
    • Google Ads (Search)
    • Facebook/Instagram Ads
    • Content Marketing / SEO
    • Referrals / Word of Mouth
    • GoHighLevel Funnels
    • Partnerships / Affiliates
    • Cold Calling
    • Trade Shows / Events

Future expansion:
    Channel cost benchmarks, integration with ad platform APIs.


MODULE B3: Cost Centers
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
    Defines WHERE money is being spent within the organizational structure,
    independent of which initiative or channel it serves.

Answers:
    "What function is this spend supporting?"
    "How much are we spending on operations vs growth?"

Examples:
    • Engineering / Development
    • Sales & Business Development
    • Marketing & Advertising
    • Operations & Admin
    • Tools & Software
    • Legal & Compliance
    • Founder Time (implicit cost)

Future expansion:
    Budget allocation per cost center, headcount planning.


MODULE B4: Strategic Goals & OKRs
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
    High-level objectives that initiatives roll up to. Provides the "north star"
    that justifies why initiatives exist.

Answers:
    "What are we trying to achieve this quarter/year?"
    "Are our initiatives aligned with our goals?"

Examples:
    • "Reach $10K MRR by Q4 2026"
    • "Acquire 50 agency clients in 2026"
    • "Launch 2 SaaS products by end of 2027"
    • "Achieve profitability on Mobile App Agency"

Future expansion:
    OKR cascading, goal-initiative mapping, progress tracking.


================================================================================
GROUP C — EXECUTION (How and When)
================================================================================

MODULE C1: Experiments
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
    Structured tests within an initiative. An experiment has a hypothesis,
    a budget, a timeframe, and measurable success criteria.

Answers:
    "What are we testing?"
    "Did the test work?"
    "What did we learn?"

Examples:
    • "Test: LinkedIn outreach with case study vs without — 2 weeks, $200 budget"
    • "Test: Landing page A vs B for SaaS signup — 1 month, $500 ad spend"
    • "Test: Cold email subject line variations — 1 week, 500 emails"

Future expansion:
    A/B test tracking, statistical significance, experiment templates.


MODULE C2: Campaigns (BOS Context)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
    BOS-level campaign tracking that BRIDGES to (but does not replace) the
    existing CRM Campaign module. Adds strategic context: which initiative,
    which channel, which experiment, expected ROI.

Answers:
    "Which initiative does this CRM campaign serve?"
    "What is the expected return on this campaign spend?"

Note:
    Your existing CRM Campaign module (leads, tags, outreach) remains unchanged.
    BOS Campaign adds the strategic wrapper.

Future expansion:
    Auto-sync with CRM campaigns, multi-channel campaign orchestration.


MODULE C3: Projects (Delivery)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
    Tracks delivery work AFTER a client is acquired — not lead generation,
    but fulfillment. Links revenue back to the initiative that generated the client.

Answers:
    "What work are we delivering?"
    "Is this project profitable?"

Examples:
    • "Build e-commerce app for Client X — $15,000 project"
    • "Implement AI chatbot for Client Y — $8,000 project"

Note:
    This is NOT a project management tool (no task boards, no Gantt charts).
    It tracks project economics and links them to BOS initiatives.

Future expansion:
    Integration with external PM tools (Asana, Linear), profitability tracking.


================================================================================
GROUP D — MEASUREMENT (How Much and How Well)
================================================================================

MODULE D1: Investment Tracker
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
    Aggregates ALL investments (money, time, resources) attributed to an
    initiative, channel, or experiment. Reads from Expenses and other modules
    via attribution — does not duplicate financial records.

Answers:
    "How much have I invested in this initiative?"
    "Where is the money going?"

Investment types:
    • Financial (expenses, ad spend, tools, salaries)
    • Time (founder hours, contractor hours)
    • Resources (existing assets deployed)

Future expansion:
    Budget vs actual, investment forecasting, burn rate alerts.


MODULE D2: Revenue Attribution
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
    Links revenue (from invoices, payments, subscriptions) back to the initiative
    and channel that generated the client/deal.

Answers:
    "How much revenue did this initiative produce?"
    "Which channel generates the most revenue?"

Future expansion:
    Multi-touch attribution, recurring revenue tracking, LTV by channel.


MODULE D3: KPI Engine
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
    Computes, stores, and surfaces all business KPIs automatically from
    attributed data. See Document 08 for full KPI list.

Answers:
    "What is the ROI?"
    "What is the CAC?"
    "What is the conversion rate at each funnel stage?"

Future expansion:
    Custom KPI definitions, alerting, benchmarking.


MODULE D4: Funnel Analytics
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
    Tracks conversion through the business funnel stages defined in Document 07:
    Investment → Lead → Meeting → Proposal → Client → Revenue → ROI

Answers:
    "Where is the funnel leaking?"
    "What is the conversion rate at each stage?"
    "How long does the sales cycle take?"

Future expansion:
    Funnel visualization, stage-by-stage optimization recommendations.


================================================================================
GROUP E — INTELLIGENCE (What Did We Learn)
================================================================================

MODULE E1: Lessons Learned
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
    Structured capture of insights from completed or paused initiatives and
    experiments. Searchable institutional memory.

Answers:
    "What did we learn from this initiative?"
    "What should we avoid next time?"
    "What worked that we should repeat?"

Structure:
    • Context (business, initiative, channel, experiment)
    • Outcome (success, failure, partial, inconclusive)
    • Key insight (what we learned)
    • Recommendation (what to do differently)
    • Tags (searchable: channel, business type, outcome)

Future expansion:
    AI-powered lesson surfacing, similarity matching across ventures.


MODULE E2: Decision Log
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
    Records strategic decisions made during initiative lifecycle — with context,
    rationale, and expected outcome. Enables retrospective analysis.

Answers:
    "Why did we decide to kill this initiative?"
    "What was the rationale for increasing budget?"
    "Were our decisions good in hindsight?"

Examples:
    • "Decision: Increase LinkedIn ad budget from $500 to $1,500/month"
      Rationale: "Conversion rate 2x industry average after 30 days"
    • "Decision: Kill Facebook Ads experiment"
      Rationale: "Zero qualified leads after $800 spend over 6 weeks"

Future expansion:
    Decision quality scoring, pattern analysis.


MODULE E3: Playbooks & Templates
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
    Reusable strategic templates derived from successful initiatives. When
    launching Business #5, start from a proven playbook instead of scratch.

Answers:
    "How did we successfully launch the last agency?"
    "What steps should I follow for a new SaaS launch?"

Examples:
    • "Agency Launch Playbook" (derived from Mobile App Agency initiative)
    • "SaaS MVP Validation Playbook"
    • "Cold Outreach Channel Playbook"

Future expansion:
    Community playbooks, industry-specific templates, AI-generated playbooks.


================================================================================
GROUP F — INTEGRATION (Connecting the Ecosystem)
================================================================================

MODULE F1: Attribution Engine
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
    The technical-business core that links operational facts (expenses, leads,
    invoices) to BOS strategic context (initiatives, channels, experiments).
    Sidecar pattern — never modifies source records.

See Document 06 for integration details.


MODULE F2: External System Adapters
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
    Optional connectors to external platforms that feed data into the BOS.

Planned adapters:
    • GoHighLevel (leads, contacts, funnel stages, campaigns)
    • Google Ads / Meta Ads (spend data, campaign performance)
    • Stripe / payment processors (revenue data)
    • Future SaaS products built on this platform

Each adapter is independent and optional.


MODULE F3: Notification & Alert Engine
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose:
    Proactive alerts when KPIs cross thresholds or decisions are needed.

Examples:
    • "Initiative X has exceeded budget by 20%"
    • "Channel Y ROI dropped below 0% — consider pausing"
    • "No leads recorded for Initiative Z in 14 days"
    • "Experiment A reached statistical significance — review results"

Future expansion:
    Email/SMS alerts, Slack integration, weekly digest reports.

================================================================================
