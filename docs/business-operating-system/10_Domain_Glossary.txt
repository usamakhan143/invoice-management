================================================================================
BUSINESS OPERATING SYSTEM (BOS)
Document 10 — Domain Glossary
================================================================================

Authoring perspective: Principal Enterprise Software Architect
Status: Architecture phase — canonical terminology
Version: 1.0

RULE: Every term in this glossary has ONE meaning within the BOS ecosystem.
      If a term already exists in the ERP with a different meaning, the BOS
      term uses a distinct canonical name (see "Forbidden Aliases" section).

================================================================================
1. CANONICAL NAMING STANDARD
================================================================================

Prefix convention:
    Bos*     — All first-class BOS entities (e.g., BosVenture, BosInitiative)
    bos*     — Collection / module namespace (documentation only at this stage)
    ERP:*    — Existing operational module entities (unchanged names)

Forbidden in BOS domain model:
    "Business" alone     — Ambiguous (see ERP:Business vs BosVenture)
    "Campaign" alone     — Ambiguous (see ERP:Campaign vs BosCampaignLink)
    "Project" alone      — Ambiguous (see BosDeliveryEngagement vs PM tools)
    "Category" alone     — Ambiguous (see ERP:ExpenseCategory vs BosCostCenter)

Documentation may use plain English in prose; the DOMAIN MODEL must use
canonical names from this glossary.


================================================================================
2. FOUNDATION TERMS
================================================================================

TERM: Business Operating System (BOS)
    Definition: The strategic layer above operational ERP/CRM modules that
                provides context, attribution, measurement, decisions, and
                institutional memory across multiple ventures.
    Not:         An accounting system, CRM, or project management tool.

TERM: BosPortfolio
    Definition: The complete set of ventures operated by the founder or
                organization. The highest-level container in BOS.
    Synonym:     Business Portfolio (prose only)
    Not:         A single venture; not a financial portfolio in the investment sense.

TERM: BosVenture
    Definition: A distinct business the founder operates (e.g., Mobile App Agency,
                AI Automation Agency, SaaS Product X). Owns initiatives, channels,
                and venture-level KPIs.
    Canonical:   BosVenture
    Forbidden:   "Business" in code/schema (conflicts with ERP:Business)
    ERP conflict: ERP:Business = commercial entity under a Customer in CRM.
                  Completely unrelated to BosVenture. NEVER merge.

TERM: BosBusinessModel
    Definition: A reusable classification of venture economics and operating
                pattern (Agency, SaaS, Product, Marketplace, Info Product).
    Not:         A specific venture; not a revenue number.

TERM: BosBusinessUnit (future)
    Definition: A subdivision within a BosVenture (e.g., "Enterprise Division"
                within an agency). Optional hierarchy level.
    Not:         A separate venture; not ERP:Business.


================================================================================
3. STRATEGY TERMS
================================================================================

TERM: BosInitiative (Growth Initiative)
    Definition: A time-bound strategic bet within a BosVenture with hypothesis,
                budget, channels, success criteria, and measurable ROI.
    Canonical:   BosInitiative
    Synonym:     Growth Initiative (prose)
    Not:         A marketing campaign, a project, a task, an expense category.

TERM: BosStrategicGoal
    Definition: A quarterly or annual objective (OKR-style) that one or more
                initiatives support. Provides strategic alignment above initiatives.
    Not:         An initiative; not a KPI value.

TERM: BosAcquisitionChannel
    Definition: A reusable method of acquiring customers/leads (LinkedIn Outreach,
                Google Ads, Referrals, Content Marketing, GoHighLevel Funnel).
    Not:         A CRM campaign; not an ad account; not a URL.

TERM: BosCostCenter
    Definition: An organizational spend classification (Engineering, Marketing,
                Operations, Tools) independent of acquisition channel.
    Not:         ERP:ExpenseCategory (operational bookkeeping label).
                  A single expense has BOTH an ERP category AND optionally a
                  BosCostCenter via attribution. They serve different purposes.

TERM: BosHypothesis
    Definition: A testable belief driving an initiative or experiment, structured
                as: "We believe [action] will produce [outcome] because [reason]."
    Not:         A success criterion; not a KPI target.

TERM: BosOffer
    Definition: What is being sold or validated in an initiative (service package,
                SaaS tier, product SKU). Links initiative to revenue analysis.
    Missing from prior docs — REQUIRED for multi-revenue-model support.
    Not:         ERP:Product (catalog item); BosOffer is strategic positioning.

TERM: BosMarketSegment / BosICP
    Definition: Ideal Customer Profile or target market for an initiative
                (industry, company size, role, geography).
    Missing from prior docs — REQUIRED for channel/experiment analysis.
    Not:         A lead record; not a CRM segment export.


================================================================================
4. EXECUTION TERMS
================================================================================

TERM: BosExperiment
    Definition: A structured test within a BosInitiative with hypothesis, budget,
                duration, success criteria, and outcome (success/failure/inconclusive).
    Not:         An A/B test tool; not a campaign.

TERM: BosCampaignLink
    Definition: A BOS strategic wrapper that links an ERP:Campaign (CRM outreach
                campaign) to a BosInitiative, BosAcquisitionChannel, and optionally
                BosExperiment. BOS does NOT replace ERP:Campaign.
    Canonical:   BosCampaignLink
    Forbidden:   "Campaign" as a standalone BOS entity name.

TERM: ERP:Campaign
    Definition: Existing CRM module entity for outreach campaigns, tags, and lead
                assignment. Unchanged. BOS reads and links; never owns.

TERM: BosDeliveryEngagement
    Definition: Post-sale delivery work for a specific client (project economics,
                profitability tracking). NOT a task manager.
    Canonical:   BosDeliveryEngagement
    Forbidden:   "Project" alone (conflicts with PM tools and vague usage).

TERM: BosAssumption
    Definition: A explicit belief taken as true for planning (e.g., "CPL will be
                under $20"). If proven false, triggers decision/review.
    Missing from prior docs — critical for institutional memory.

TERM: BosRisk
    Definition: A identified threat to initiative success with likelihood,
                impact, and mitigation. Distinct from assumptions.
    Missing from prior docs.


================================================================================
5. ATTRIBUTION & MEASUREMENT TERMS
================================================================================

TERM: BosAttribution
    Definition: A sidecar record linking an operational fact (ERP:Expense,
                ERP:Lead, ERP:Invoice, etc.) to BOS context (initiative,
                channel, experiment, cost center) with optional allocation %.
    Rule:        NEVER modifies the source operational record.

TERM: BosAttributionRule (future)
    Definition: A configurable rule for semi-automatic attribution (e.g.,
                "Expenses with payee Google → Channel Google Ads").
    Not:         An attribution instance; not a Firestore security rule.

TERM: BosSourceModule
    Definition: Enum identifying origin of attributed record: expense, lead,
                invoice, loan, time_entry, deposit, external_adapter, etc.

TERM: BosFunnelStageEvent
    Definition: A point-in-time record that a lead/opportunity reached a funnel
                stage (Lead Generated, Meeting Held, Proposal Sent, Client Closed).
    Authority:   Stage facts may originate in CRM; BOS owns the attributed
                interpretation for initiative/channel analytics.

TERM: BosMetricDefinition
    Definition: A versioned definition of a KPI (name, formula, inputs, scope).
                Prevents "ROI changed because formula changed" confusion.
    Missing from prior docs — REQUIRED for 10-year maintainability.

TERM: BosMetricSnapshot
    Definition: A point-in-time computed KPI value for an initiative/channel/
                venture at a specific date. Enables historical reporting even
                when formulas or attributions change later.

TERM: BosInvestment (computed concept)
    Definition: Sum of attributed operational outflows minus returns. Not a
                stored authoritative amount — computed from BosAttribution +
                ERP:Expense + ERP:ExpenseReturn unless cached as BosMetricSnapshot.

TERM: BosRevenue (computed concept)
    Definition: Sum of attributed invoice/payment inflows. Computed, not stored
                as authoritative, unless cached.

TERM: ROI (Return on Investment)
    Definition: (Revenue - Investment) / Investment × 100. Always computed via
                BosMetricDefinition; never manually entered as source of truth.


================================================================================
6. INTELLIGENCE TERMS
================================================================================

TERM: BosDecision
    Definition: A recorded strategic choice with context, rationale, alternatives,
                expected outcome, and later actual outcome. Core institutional
                memory asset. See Document 13.

TERM: BosDecisionAlternative
    Definition: A option considered but not chosen when a BosDecision was made.
    Not:         A lesson learned; not an experiment.

TERM: BosLessonLearned
    Definition: Structured insight captured at initiative/experiment closure or
                after significant events. Searchable, tagged, reusable.
    Distinct from: BosDecision (a choice) vs BosLesson (an insight).

TERM: BosPlaybook
    Definition: A reusable template derived from successful initiatives containing
                recommended channels, budget ranges, steps, and linked lessons.
    Lifecycle:   Draft → Published → Deprecated (see Document 11).

TERM: BosInstitutionalMemory
    Definition: The collective set of BosDecision, BosLessonLearned, BosPlaybook,
                and BosAssumption records across the portfolio. The compounding
                asset of the BOS.


================================================================================
7. INTEGRATION TERMS
================================================================================

TERM: ERP:Expense
    Definition: Existing expense document. Operational source of financial outflow.
    BOS role:   Read + BosAttribution only.

TERM: ERP:ExpenseReturn
    Definition: Existing refund/cashback event against an expense.
    BOS role:   Read; reduces net investment in ROI calculations.

TERM: ERP:Lead
    Definition: Existing CRM lead record.
    BOS role:   Read + BosAttribution + BosFunnelStageEvent.

TERM: ERP:Invoice
    Definition: Existing invoice/revenue record.
    BOS role:   Read + revenue BosAttribution.

TERM: ERP:Customer / ERP:Business (CRM)
    Definition: Customer and sub-entity in CRM. ERP:Business = client's business,
                NOT founder's venture.
    BOS role:   Read for revenue linkage. NEVER rename or overload.

TERM: ExternalAdapter
    Definition: Optional connector (GoHighLevel, Google Ads, Stripe) that imports
                data into BOS attribution layer. Replaceable. Never embedded in core.

TERM: Enrichment
    Definition: Adding BOS columns/context to Reports/Dashboard without modifying
                existing calculations or outputs.


================================================================================
8. PERMISSION & ACCESS TERMS
================================================================================

TERM: Operational Role
    Definition: ERP permissions (expenses_create, leads_view, etc.). Unchanged.

TERM: BOS Role
    Definition: Strategic permissions (bos_initiatives_manage, bos_attributions_manage,
                bos_decisions_manage). Separate namespace from operational roles.

TERM: Attribution Coverage
    Definition: % of operational records in scope that have active BosAttribution.
                Data quality KPI, not a permission.


================================================================================
9. FORBIDDEN ALIASES — QUICK REFERENCE
================================================================================

    Never use in BOS schema     | Use instead              | Why
    ----------------------------|--------------------------|---------------------------
    Business                    | BosVenture               | CRM ERP:Business conflict
    Campaign (BOS entity)       | BosCampaignLink          | ERP:Campaign conflict
    Project                     | BosDeliveryEngagement    | PM tool confusion
    Category (strategic)        | BosCostCenter            | ERP:ExpenseCategory
    Tag (CRM)                   | ERP:CampaignTag          | Different from BosTag
    Return (generic)            | ERP:ExpenseReturn        | Specific event type
    BI Layer                    | BOS Layer                | Single canonical name
    Investment (stored field)   | Computed via attribution | Source of truth rule
