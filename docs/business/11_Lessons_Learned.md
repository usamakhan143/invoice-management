# Lessons Learned

> **Status:** Template — capture learnings as they happen. BOS `BosLessonLearned` (Phase 2+) will support initiative closure; use this doc for broader business learnings.

---

## How to use

1. Add an entry after projects, campaigns, failed deals, or major decisions.
2. Be specific: what happened, why, what to repeat or avoid.
3. Tag entries so patterns emerge over time.
4. Review quarterly in founder KPI ritual (`09_KPIs.md`).
