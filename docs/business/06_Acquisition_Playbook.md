# Acquisition Playbook

> **Status:** Template — reusable channel structure only. Fill tactics when ready.

## How to use this playbook

Each channel section follows the same structure:

1. **Purpose** — Why we use this channel  
2. **ICP fit** — Who it reaches best  
3. **Offer fit** — What we promote  
4. **Process** — Steps from first touch to qualified lead  
5. **Tools** — Stack and assets  
6. **Metrics** — What to measure  
7. **Budget** — Spend rules (if applicable)  
8. **Owner** — Who runs it  
9. **Status** — Active / testing / paused
